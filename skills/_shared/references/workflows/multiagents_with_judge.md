# Judge supervision overlay

## Judge session and consultation

Apply `autonomy-policy.md`. Judge is an optional DSH assignment selected by
effective settings or an explicit request. No fixed model, reasoning level,
participant count or irreplaceable session is required. A fresh session can use
durable prior assessments; loss of a session does not invalidate completed work.

Provide objective, current owner, relevant completed work/evidence, trajectory,
proposed route and the question. Include precise source locators when useful;
no fixed packet schema is required. Judge is read-only and returns
`SUPPORT|REDIRECT|ESCALATE_OPERATOR`, its evidence-based reason and proposed
owner/conditions. This assessment is not a check verdict or permission.

`SUPPORT` does not approve a route on the user's behalf. `REDIRECT` is a proposal,
not a launch. Disagreement, escalation and main-path consultation use the common
decision flow. Preserve only the assessment and accepted decision needed for
continuation at the existing owner.

## autonomous with judge

When selected, Judge can assess Foundation decisions, the prepared product queue,
contradictory evidence, repeated REJECT, lack of progress, owning-layer drift,
or materially different recovery routes. Disabled Judge adds no gate or report.

## autopilot with judge

When selected, Judge can assess retry disposition and wave progress. Unknown
cause may warrant `/debug`; a non-trivial correction may warrant
`/technical-premortem`; changed accepted semantics returns to its planning
owner. The host retains launch, budget, lifecycle and recovery mechanics.
