# /grill-me — Product discovery

Apply `.memory-bank/workflows/autonomy-policy.md` for decisions, effective
settings, host ownership and recovery.

Stress-test the uncertain idea until its intent, audience, useful outcome and
scope are clear enough for product work. Read an existing
`.memory-bank/analysis/brief.md` and relevant project evidence; legacy
`product-brief.md` or brainstorming records are migration context. Apply the
installed `creator-vibe` lens when taste, experience or implicit intent matters.

Find discoverable facts from evidence rather than interviewing the user for
them. Separate facts, recommendations, accepted choices and unknowns. Questions
and confirmation of shared understanding use the common decision flow; autonomous
may settle semantic choices while unavailable facts remain explicit gaps.
No fixed questionnaire, frontier sequence or answer format is required.

Keep `.memory-bank/analysis/brief.md` and its analysis index durable as useful
findings/decisions change. Cover intent, users/problem/outcome, product direction,
MVP/non-goals, sources, material constraints and open questions without empty
sections or a transcript. Preserve reasons for rejected directions only when
needed to avoid reopening them.

Use `type: product-brief`, `status: draft` and `decision: blocked` while material
discovery remains unresolved. When shared understanding is accepted through the
common flow, use `status: active`, `decision: proceed`; keep the final Decision
section consistent and identify user/autonomous provenance honestly.

A ready brief returns `/write-prd` if principles are `ratified|partial|skipped`,
otherwise `/constitution`. A blocked brief stays with `/grill-me`, naming the
actual unresolved input rather than launching downstream work.
