# /discuss — Bounded consultation

Apply `.memory-bank/workflows/autonomy-policy.md` for decisions, effective
settings, host ownership and recovery.

Resolve the material question in `$ARGUMENTS` or the current operation using
relevant governing evidence. This is a consultation on the main path even if
its next operation will not change. Do not ask decorative questions or treat a
recommendation, silence or an incomplete answer as approval.

Return the concrete question/proposal through the common decision flow. Do not
require a new artifact or route change merely to ask it. Identify the existing
canonical owner where the accepted answer affects continuation; apply it there
within the assignment, or return it to that owner for its normal write. If that
artifact does not yet exist, the owner creates its normal result, not an
interview registry or extra decision-log family.

Remove superseded contradictions when applying a decision. Preserve material
unknowns, affected scope and user/autonomous provenance. Do not independently
change task lifecycle, product scope or another owner's contract.

Return to the invoking owner with the concise decision and its durable location
or required owner update. Dependent work resumes only after the actual decision
and necessary input exist; independent authorized analysis may continue. No
full downstream chain or extra approval is implied by the consultation itself.
